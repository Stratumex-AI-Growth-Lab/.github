# Stratumex — AI Growth Lab 🧪✨

### *Accelerating the next generation of AI-driven products.*

Welcome to the **Stratumex AI Growth Lab**. We are a venture studio and research collective dedicated to building, rebuilding, and scaling intelligent systems. Our mission is to bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI research and practical, high-performance consumer applications.

---

## 🛠 What We're Building

At Stratumex, we focus on "Growth-First" engineering—creating tools that don't just solve problems, but scale with the users.

* **[cue-it-now](https://github.com/Stratumex-AI-Growth-Lab/cue-it-now)**: Our flagship modernization project. A high-performance queuing and scheduling rebuild designed for modern SaaS architectures.
* **AI Implementation Patterns**: Developing reusable frameworks for LLM orchestration and vector database integration.
* **Stealth Projects**: We currently have two internal AI agents in development focusing on automated growth hacking and data synthesis.
