# Stratumex — AI Growth Lab 🧪✨

### *Bridging the gap between AI research and production-ready systems.*

Welcome to the **Stratumex AI Growth Lab**. We are a specialized venture studio and research collective dedicated to building, rebuilding, and scaling intelligent systems. We focus on **"Growth-First" engineering**—creating AI tools that don't just solve problems, but scale seamlessly with user demand.

---

## 🛠 What We're Building

We prioritize high-performance, modular AI systems built on cloud-native principles.

* **[cue-it-now](https://github.com/Stratumex-AI-Growth-Lab/cue-it-now)**: Our flagship modernization project. A high-performance queuing and scheduling rebuild designed for modern SaaS architectures.
* **AI Implementation Patterns**: Developing reusable frameworks for LLM orchestration, agentic workflows, and vector database integration.
* **Stealth Agents**: We are currently developing internal AI agents focused on automated growth hacking and complex data synthesis.
